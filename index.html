Ayush Arya Kashyap

I'm a computer science student at IIIT Bangalore, currently in Singapore as a visiting researcher at SUTD. I work on getting language models to find things that shouldn't be there: bugs and vulnerabilities in software and in machine-learning systems.

Before this I built agentic AI tooling for network operations at Microsoft, shipped production AI apps at Trimble, ran neuroimaging research at IIIT Bangalore, and won the SEBI National Securities Market Hackathon.

What I'm working on now

LLM-guided fuzzing of serialized ML models. Model files like pickles can carry executable payloads. At SUTD I'm reproducing systems such as ShadowPickle, PickleFuzzer and DynaHug/DynaOrg, building Docker-based baselines, and exploring how semantic-aware mutation, constraint solving and LLM reasoning can push fuzzers toward deeper vulnerabilities in binary and serialized formats.

More broadly I'm drawn to AI that actually does things — agents that reason, use tools and act on other systems — and to what goes wrong when you give models more autonomy. Fintech and fraud detection are my other recurring rabbit hole.
